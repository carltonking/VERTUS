What Alfred does

One assistant for your whole digital life

Not another chatbot you have to babysit. Alfred takes real actions across the tools you already use.

Email, handled

Drafts and sends replies that sound exactly like you. Clears your inbox, flags what needs a human, and never misses a thread.

Texts, answered

Keeps your conversations warm — replying to messages, confirming plans, and following up so nothing slips through the cracks.

Calendar, managed

Schedules, reschedules, and protects your time. Tell Alfred what you need and the invites, reminders, and prep just appear.

Learns your voice

The more you use it, the more it sounds like you — your phrasing, your tone, who you're formal with and who you're not.

Knows your people

Remembers your contacts, relationships, and history — so it always replies with the right context to the right person.

Private by design

Your data is yours. Alfred works on your machine and asks before it does anything that leaves a mark.
